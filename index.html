    How positions are asked when a note appears more than once
    • Any position: every spot with that note name counts.
    • Every position: click all of them in the area (fretboard clicks only).
    • Exact pitch: the note with its octave number, like G3 or E4. This is the clearest way to point at one specific pitch: it never changes meaning when you change the area, and it teaches you where the octaves sit. If the same pitch exists on two strings (E4 is open e and also B string fret 5), either one is right.
    • Nearest note (its own quiz): you choose the first starting spot by clicking or playing it; after that each question starts from the spot you should have found. The starting spot is marked on the neck and you find the closest spot in your area with the asked note, measured in frets and strings. With Direction set to “higher or lower”, every question asks for the nearest one that sounds higher (or lower) than where you are; otherwise direction is only given when the asked note is the same as the starting note, meaning the next octave that way. Ties all count.
    • Lowest / 2nd lowest / highest: counted by pitch inside the selected area, so “2nd lowest G” depends on the area you picked.

    Keys: Space starts or stops, 1–9 picks an answer, R replays the note.
